YooFree is an open, ad-free client for Yoosee/GW cameras on Android.

It talks directly to the camera on your local network: no cloud, no account, no AI, no telemetry. Just you and your camera.

Features:
- Live HEVC/H.265 1080p video straight from the camera
- PTZ: move the camera (up/down/left/right)
- Digital zoom from 1x to 5x
- Local MP4 recording (no re-encode, no cloud)
- Rotate the image 90 degrees (useful when the camera is upside down)
- Landscape mode with auto-hiding HUD, two-finger pinch zoom, camera audio, and automatic camera discovery on the network

Only the camera IP is needed. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the camera.

YooFree is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. For support or bug reports, use the GitHub issue tracker.
